How it works

Three moves. Every day.

1

Pick your non-negotiables

Training, cold showers, reading, deep work, no morning doomscrolling: up to 15 daily habits with your own icons, colours and reminders.

2

Show up and tick them off

Every habit earns XP. A daily quest adds a bonus, and the home-screen widget lets you tick habits without opening the app.

3

Hold to lock in

Seal the day into your matrix. Your streak grows, your rank climbs, and badges land at 10, 30, 60 and 90 days.
