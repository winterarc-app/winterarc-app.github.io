Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026 · Android app com.arc90.app

Summary. Winter Arc has no accounts and no ads. Everything you enter stays on your device. To fix crashes and improve the app, it sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, never what you type, and you can turn them off in Profile.

Data you enter

To work, the app stores what you type into it: your callsign, gender, age, height and body weight; your habits and which ones you completed; daily logs (water, sleep, calories and macros, steps, reading, mindfulness and focus minutes); workouts and sets; goals; journal reflections and energy ratings; progress photos you add; and app settings such as theme and units.

Where it is stored

This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, and the developer cannot see it.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To find and fix crashes and to see which features people use, the app sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics through Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics):

  • Crash reports: what went wrong in the app's code, plus the device model, Android version and app version.
  • Usage statistics: which screens and features are used, and simple counts such as the day of your arc or the length of a streak, tied to a random ID for this installation.

Never sent: your name or callsign, habit names, notes, reflections, weights, photos, or anything else you type. The app does not use the Advertising ID and shows no ads.

Google processes this data under the Firebase terms and Google's privacy policy. Crash reports are kept for 90 days; usage statistics for 2 months.

You can turn both off at any time: Profile → at the very bottom, "Anonymous usage stats & crash reports".

Backups you make

If you use "Backup & Restore", the app writes your data to a backup file. You choose where it goes: a folder you pick on your device or in a cloud drive, or an app you send it to with the Android share sheet. From then on the file is handled by that place or app under its own terms. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

When you restore, the app reads only the backup file you choose. Before restoring, it keeps a copy of the data it replaces in its private storage so you can undo once.

If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is run by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Progress photos

Photos you take or pick for progress tracking are copied into the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere and are excluded from Android device backup. A photo leaves your device only if you share a collage yourself, or save or send a backup file with "Include progress photos" on: then the photos are in that file, which goes only where you choose. The app uses the system camera and photo picker and does not request access to your whole gallery.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device with Android's notification system. They are created from your data on the device and are never sent through a server. You can change or turn them off in Profile → Reminders or in Android settings.

Deleting your data

Erase everything at any time from Home → Streak → Restart Arc → Erase Everything, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Health Disclaimer

Not medical advice

Winter Arc is a self-tracking and motivation tool for general wellness. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Targets are generic estimates

Suggested water, calorie, protein, carb and sleep targets, training weights, progression hints and one-rep-max estimates are calculated from simple formulas. They are not personalized recommendations and may not be right for you.

Talk to a professional first

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a new exercise program, diet, calorie restriction, cold exposure or breathwork practice — especially if you are pregnant, under 18, have a medical condition, take medication, or have a history of eating disorders.

Listen to your body

Stop immediately and seek medical help if you feel pain, dizziness, chest tightness, shortness of breath or any other worrying symptom. Streaks and ranks are never worth an injury. You use the app at your own risk.
